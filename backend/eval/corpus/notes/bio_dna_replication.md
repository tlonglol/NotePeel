## DNA Structure
Double helix, antiparallel strands (5' to 3' and 3' to 5'). Sugar phosphate backbone, nitrogen bases pair A-T (2 H bonds) and G-C (3 H bonds). Watson & Crick 1953, Franklin's xray data.

## Replication is Semiconservative
Each new molecule has 1 old strand + 1 new strand. Meselson-Stahl experiment proved this w/ nitrogen isotopes.

## Enzymes (know these!!)
- Helicase - unzips the double helix, breaks H bonds, creates replication fork
- Single strand binding proteins - keep strands apart
- Primase - lays down RNA primer so polymerase has a place to start
- DNA polymerase III - adds nucleotides 5' -> 3' only, proofreads
- Leading strand made continuously, lagging strand in Okazaki fragments
- DNA polymerase I - replaces RNA primers with DNA
- Ligase - glues Okazaki fragments together
- Topoisomerase - relieves tension ahead of the fork
