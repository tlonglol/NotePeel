## Photosynthesis
The process by which plants convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose. Happens in the chloroplast. Overall reaction: $6CO_2 + 6H_2O + light \rightarrow C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2$

## Light Dependent Reactions
- occur in the thylakoid membrane
- chlorophyll absorbs light, excites electrons
- water is split (photolysis) -> releases $O_2$ as a byproduct
- electron transport chain pumps $H^+$ into thylakoid space
- ATP synthase makes ATP, NADP+ reduced to NADPH
- products: ATP and NADPH, used in the Calvin cycle

## Calvin Cycle (light independent)
- occurs in the stroma
- carbon fixation: rubisco attaches $CO_2$ to RuBP (5 carbon sugar)
- uses ATP and NADPH from light rxns to reduce 3-PGA to G3P
- G3P used to build glucose, RuBP regenerated
- 6 turns of the cycle = 1 glucose
