## Origins
US vs USSR after WWII - capitalism vs communism, no direct fighting (thats why its "cold"). Yalta and Potsdam conferences split Germany and Berlin into 4 zones. Iron Curtain speech by Churchill 1946. Soviets set up satellite states in Eastern Europe.

## Containment
Policy of stopping the SPREAD of communism (George Kennan's long telegram). Not rollback, just hold the line.
- Truman Doctrine 1947 - aid to Greece and Turkey, US will support free peoples resisting communism
- Marshall Plan 1948 - $13 billion to rebuild Western Europe so communism wouldnt look appealing
- Berlin Airlift 1948-49 - Soviets blockade West Berlin, US flies in supplies for 11 months
- NATO 1949 vs Warsaw Pact 1955
- Korean War 1950-53 - containment in action, ended at the 38th parallel where it started

## Flashpoints
- arms race, hydrogen bomb, Sputnik 1957 -> space race
- Bay of Pigs 1961 failed invasion of Cuba
- Cuban Missile Crisis Oct 1962 - 13 days, closest to nuclear war, Khrushchev removes missiles, US secretly removes missiles from Turkey
- Vietnam - domino theory, escalation under Johnson, ended 1975
- ended w/ fall of the Berlin Wall 1989 and USSR dissolving 1991
