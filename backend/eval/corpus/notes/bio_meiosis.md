## Meiosis Overview
Produces gametes (sperm & egg). 2 rounds of division, 1 round of DNA replication. Starts w/ 1 diploid cell -> ends w/ 4 haploid cells (n) that are genetically different. This is the source of genetic variation.

## Meiosis I (reductional)
- Prophase I - homologous chromosomes pair up (synapsis) forming tetrads, crossing over happens here! chiasmata = points of exchange
- Metaphase I - tetrads line up at the plate, independent assortment (random orientation)
- Anaphase I - homologous pairs separate (sister chromatids stay together)
- Telophase I - 2 haploid cells

## Meiosis II
- basically like mitosis but with haploid cells
- sister chromatids separate in anaphase II
- end result 4 haploid cells

## Mitosis vs Meiosis
- mitosis: 1 division, 2 identical diploid cells, body cells
- meiosis: 2 divisions, 4 unique haploid cells, gametes only
- nondisjunction = chromosomes fail to separate -> trisomy 21 (down syndrome)
