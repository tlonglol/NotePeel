## Long Term Causes
Sectionalism - North (industrial, free labor, tariffs) vs South (agricultural, slavery, cotton). States rights argument: could states nullify federal law? Slavery was the core issue underneath all of it. Missouri Compromise 1820 - Missouri slave, Maine free, no slavery north of 36 30 line.

## 1850s Escalation
- Compromise of 1850: California free, popular sovereignty in Utah/NM, stronger Fugitive Slave Act (angered the north)
- Uncle Toms Cabin 1852 - Stowe, turned northern opinion against slavery
- Kansas Nebraska Act 1854 - repealed Missouri Compromise, popular sovereignty -> Bleeding Kansas violence
- Dred Scott 1857 - Supreme Court said slaves are property, Congress cant ban slavery in territories
- John Browns raid on Harpers Ferry 1859 - south terrified of slave revolt

## Immediate Cause
Lincoln elected Nov 1860 w/ no southern electoral votes (Republican, against expansion of slavery not abolition at first). South Carolina secedes Dec 1860, 6 more follow, form the Confederacy w/ Jefferson Davis. Fort Sumter April 1861 - first shots, 4 more states secede.
