## Roots in the Treaty of Versailles
Germany humiliated: war guilt clause, huge reparations, lost territory, army capped at 100k. Hyperinflation 1923 then the Great Depression -> desperation, extremist parties gained support. Weimar Republic seen as weak.

## Rise of Totalitarian Leaders
- Hitler and the Nazis, chancellor Jan 1933, Enabling Act -> dictatorship. Promised to overturn Versailles and unite German speakers (lebensraum)
- Mussolini, fascist Italy, invaded Ethiopia 1935
- Japan - militarists, invaded Manchuria 1931, China 1937
- League of Nations did nothing (no army, US not a member)

## Appeasement
Britain & France gave in to avoid war. Germany remilitarized the Rhineland 1936, annexed Austria (Anschluss) 1938. Munich Conference Sept 1938 - Chamberlain gives Hitler the Sudetenland, "peace for our time". Hitler takes the rest of Czechoslovakia March 1939 anyway. Lesson everyone drew: appeasement fails.

## The Start
Nazi-Soviet nonaggression pact Aug 1939 (secretly split Poland). Germany invades Poland Sept 1 1939, Britain and France declare war Sept 3. US enters after Pearl Harbor Dec 7 1941.
