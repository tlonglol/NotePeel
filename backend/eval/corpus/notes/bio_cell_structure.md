## Cell Structure
Cells are the basic structural and functional unit of all living organisms. Cell theory: all living things are made of cells, cells come from pre-existing cells, the cell is the smallest unit of life.

## Key Organelles
- Nucleus - stores DNA and controls cell activities, surrounded by nuclear envelope w/ pores
- Mitochondria - the powerhouse, produces ATP via cellular respiration, has its own DNA
- Ribosomes - synthesize proteins, free in cytoplasm or attached to rough ER
- Endoplasmic reticulum - rough ER (ribosomes, protein) vs smooth ER (lipids, detox)
- Golgi apparatus - packages and ships proteins in vesicles
- Lysosomes - digestive enzymes, break down waste (animal cells)
- Chloroplasts - photosynthesis, plants only
- Cell wall - cellulose in plants, rigid support

## Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic
- Prokaryotes - no membrane bound nucleus, DNA floats in nucleoid region, ex bacteria
- Eukaryotes - membrane bound nucleus and organelles, plants animals fungi protists
- both have ribosomes, cell membrane, cytoplasm
