## MAIN Causes
- Militarism - arms race, esp Britain vs Germany naval race (dreadnoughts), huge standing armies, war plans ready (Schlieffen Plan)
- Alliances - Triple Entente (France, Russia, Britain) vs Triple Alliance (Germany, Austria-Hungary, Italy). a local war pulled everyone in
- Imperialism - competition for colonies in Africa and Asia, Moroccan crises
- Nationalism - Serbian nationalism in the Balkans, pan-Slavism backed by Russia, French revenge for Alsace-Lorraine

## The Spark
June 28 1914 - Archduke Franz Ferdinand (heir to Austria-Hungary) assassinated in Sarajevo by Gavrilo Princip, a Bosnian Serb from the Black Hand. Austria gives Serbia an ultimatum, declares war July 28. Russia mobilizes to back Serbia, Germany declares war on Russia then France, invades neutral Belgium -> Britain declares war on Germany Aug 4.

## Why the US Entered (1917)
- unrestricted submarine warfare, sinking of the Lusitania 1915 (128 Americans)
- Zimmermann telegram - Germany offers Mexico US territory
- Wilson: "make the world safe for democracy"
- Treaty of Versailles 1919 ended it, war guilt clause on Germany, League of Nations (US never joined)
