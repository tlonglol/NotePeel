## Cell Cycle
Interphase (G1, S, G2) then M phase. G1 = growth, S = DNA synthesis (chromosomes replicate), G2 = prep for division. Checkpoints control the cycle, p53 stops it if DNA is damaged. Cancer = cell cycle out of control.

## Phases of Mitosis (PMAT)
- Prophase - chromatin condenses into chromosomes, nuclear envelope breaks down, spindle fibers form from centrioles
- Metaphase - chromosomes line up at the metaphase plate (middle of the cell)
- Anaphase - sister chromatids pulled apart to opposite poles by spindle
- Telophase - nuclear envelopes reform, chromosomes uncoil
- Cytokinesis - cytoplasm divides, cleavage furrow in animal cells, cell plate in plants

## Result
- 2 identical daughter cells, diploid (2n), same as parent
- used for growth, repair, asexual reproduction
- humans: 46 chromosomes -> 2 cells w/ 46 each
