## Cellular Respiration
Breaks down glucose to make ATP. Opposite of photosynthesis basically. Equation: $C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2 \rightarrow 6CO_2 + 6H_2O + ATP$. Aerobic = needs oxygen. Total yield approx 30 to 32 ATP per glucose (textbook says 36-38 but thats the old number)

## Three Stages
- Glycolysis - in cytoplasm, glucose (6C) split into 2 pyruvate (3C), net 2 ATP + 2 NADH, no oxygen needed
- Krebs cycle (citric acid cycle) - in mitochondrial matrix, pyruvate -> acetyl CoA first, produces 2 ATP, 6 NADH, 2 FADH2, releases $CO_2$
- Electron transport chain - inner mitochondrial membrane, NADH & FADH2 donate electrons, oxygen is the final electron acceptor -> forms water, makes ~26-28 ATP by chemiosmosis

## Anaerobic
- fermentation when no O2 available
- lactic acid fermentation in muscles (thats the burn)
- alcoholic fermentation in yeast -> ethanol + CO2
- only 2 ATP total (just glycolysis)
