## Causes
- stock market crash Oct 29 1929 (Black Tuesday) - buying on margin, speculation bubble
- bank failures - no deposit insurance, runs on banks, 9000 banks failed
- overproduction and underconsumption, uneven wealth distribution
- Smoot-Hawley tariff 1930 killed international trade
- Dust Bowl in the plains - drought + bad farming, Okies migrate to California
- Hoover: believed in voluntarism and rugged individualism, too little too late, Hoovervilles

## FDR and the New Deal
Elected 1932, "New Deal" = Relief, Recovery, Reform (the 3 Rs). First 100 days flood of legislation. Fireside chats on the radio. Bank holiday to stop the runs.

## Key Programs (alphabet agencies)
- CCC - jobs for young men planting trees, parks
- WPA - built roads, bridges, schools, hired artists and writers
- TVA - dams and electricity in the Tennessee valley
- FDIC - insures bank deposits (still exists)
- SEC - regulates the stock market
- Social Security Act 1935 - pensions for the elderly, unemployment insurance
- AAA paid farmers to not grow crops (struck down by the court)

## Did It Work
reduced suffering and reformed the system but unemployment stayed high (~15%) until WWII spending ended the depression. expanded the role of federal government permanently.
