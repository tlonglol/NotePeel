## Work
$W = Fd\cos\theta$, only the component of force along displacement does work. Units joules (J). Work is zero if force is perpendicular to motion (carrying a box horizontally, centripetal force). Negative work when force opposes motion (friction).

## Kinetic and Potential Energy
- $KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$
- gravitational $PE = mgh$ (near earth surface)
- spring $PE = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$, Hookes law $F = -kx$
- work energy theorem: $W_{net} = \Delta KE$

## Conservation of Energy
$KE_i + PE_i = KE_f + PE_f$ if only conservative forces (gravity, spring). With friction: $E_i = E_f + W_{friction}$, energy lost as heat. Roller coaster: at the top mostly PE, at the bottom mostly KE, total constant.

## Power
$P = \frac{W}{t} = Fv$, rate of doing work. Units watts (W) = J/s. 1 horsepower = 746 W.
