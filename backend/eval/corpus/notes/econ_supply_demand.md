## Demand
Law of demand: price up -> quantity demanded down (downward sloping curve), ceteris paribus. Movement ALONG the curve = price change. SHIFT of the curve = something else changed.

## Demand Shifters (TRIBE)
- Tastes and preferences
- Related goods: substitutes (price of Pepsi up -> demand for Coke up), complements (price of hot dogs up -> demand for buns down)
- Income: normal goods (income up, demand up) vs inferior goods (income up, demand down, ex ramen, bus rides)
- Buyers (number of)
- Expectations about future prices

## Supply
Law of supply: price up -> quantity supplied up (upward sloping). Shifters: input costs, technology, number of sellers, taxes/subsidies, expectations, prices of related outputs.

## Equilibrium
Where supply = demand, market clearing price and quantity. Surplus when price is above equilibrium (Qs > Qd) -> price falls. Shortage when price is below (Qd > Qs) -> price rises. Price ceiling (rent control) below eq -> shortage. Price floor (minimum wage) above eq -> surplus. Double shift: if both curves move, either price or quantity is indeterminate.
