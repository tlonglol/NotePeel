## Newtons 3 Laws
- 1st (inertia): object stays at rest or constant velocity unless a net force acts on it. mass is a measure of inertia
- 2nd: $\sum F = ma$, net force = mass x acceleration, units N = kg m/s^2
- 3rd: every action has an equal and opposite reaction, forces come in pairs acting on DIFFERENT objects

## Free Body Diagrams
- draw all forces on ONE object as arrows from the center
- weight $W = mg$ always down, g = 9.8 m/s^2
- normal force perpendicular to surface
- tension along the rope
- friction opposes motion (or attempted motion)
- on an incline: split gravity into $mg\sin\theta$ (along) and $mg\cos\theta$ (perp)

## Friction
- static friction $f_s \leq \mu_s N$, holds until it breaks
- kinetic friction $f_k = \mu_k N$, $\mu_k < \mu_s$ usually
- coefficient depends on the surfaces, NOT on area

## Common Mistake
normal force is not always equal to mg! only on flat ground w/ no other vertical forces. elevator accelerating up -> N > mg
