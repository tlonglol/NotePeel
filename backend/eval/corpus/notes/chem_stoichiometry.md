## The Mole
1 mole = $6.022 \times 10^{23}$ particles (Avogadro's number). Molar mass in g/mol from periodic table, ex $H_2O$ = 2(1.01) + 16.00 = 18.02 g/mol. At STP 1 mol of gas = 22.4 L.

## Mole Road Map
grams -> moles (divide by molar mass) -> moles of other substance (mole ratio from balanced eqn) -> grams (multiply by molar mass). ALWAYS balance the equation first!

## Limiting Reactant
- the reactant that runs out first, limits how much product forms
- find moles of product each reactant could make, smallest wins
- excess reactant = whats left over
- theoretical yield = max product from limiting reactant
- percent yield = $\frac{actual}{theoretical} \times 100$

## Example
$2H_2 + O_2 \rightarrow 2H_2O$. 4 g H2 = 2 mol, 32 g O2 = 1 mol. ratio needs 2:1 so exactly enough, neither limiting. makes 2 mol water = 36 g
