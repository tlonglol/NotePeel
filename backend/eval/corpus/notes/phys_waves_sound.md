## Wave Basics
- $v = f\lambda$, speed = frequency x wavelength
- period $T = 1/f$, frequency in Hz
- transverse: particles move perpendicular to wave (light, string)
- longitudinal: particles move parallel (sound, compressions & rarefactions)
- amplitude relates to energy / loudness, frequency to pitch

## Wave Behavior
- reflection, refraction (bending when speed changes), diffraction (spreading around obstacles)
- interference: constructive (in phase, bigger) and destructive (out of phase, cancel)
- standing waves: nodes (no motion) and antinodes, string fixed both ends $\lambda_n = \frac{2L}{n}$
- resonance when driving frequency matches natural frequency

## Sound
- speed in air approx 343 m/s at 20 C, faster in solids
- Doppler effect: source moving toward you -> higher frequency (pitch), moving away -> lower. ambulance siren
- $f' = f\frac{v \pm v_o}{v \mp v_s}$
- decibels: logarithmic scale, +10 dB = 10x intensity
- beats: two close frequencies, $f_{beat} = |f_1 - f_2|$
