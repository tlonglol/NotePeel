## Plans for Reconstruction (1865 to 1877)
- Lincolns 10% plan - lenient, state readmitted when 10% of voters take loyalty oath
- Andrew Johnson - continued lenient approach, pardoned confederates, vetoed civil rights bills, impeached 1868 (acquitted by 1 vote)
- Radical Republicans - wanted to punish the south and protect freedmen, Reconstruction Acts 1867 put the south under military districts

## Reconstruction Amendments
- 13th (1865) - abolished slavery
- 14th (1868) - citizenship to everyone born in the US, equal protection, due process
- 15th (1870) - voting cant be denied based on race (men only)

## Freedmens Bureau
federal agency to help former slaves w/ food, schools, legal help. built schools and colleges. underfunded, ended 1872.

## End of Reconstruction and Its Failure
Southern resistance: black codes, KKK violence, sharecropping trapped freedmen in debt. Compromise of 1877 - Hayes gets presidency, federal troops leave the south. Then Jim Crow laws, poll taxes, literacy tests, Plessy v Ferguson 1896 "separate but equal". Political rights gained on paper were lost in practice for almost 100 years.
