## Momentum
$p = mv$, vector, units kg m/s. Impulse $J = F \Delta t = \Delta p$, area under force vs time graph. Thats why airbags work - same $\Delta p$ but longer $\Delta t$ means smaller force.

## Conservation of Momentum
Total momentum of a closed system is conserved (no external forces). $m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2 = m_1 v_1' + m_2 v_2'$. Works in each direction separately (x and y).

## Types of Collisions
- Elastic - momentum AND kinetic energy conserved, objects bounce off, ex billiard balls, gas molecules
- Inelastic - momentum conserved, KE is NOT (lost to heat, sound, deformation)
- Perfectly inelastic - objects stick together, $m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2 = (m_1 + m_2) v'$, max KE lost
- explosions are the reverse, start at rest, momentum still zero total

## Center of Mass
$x_{cm} = \frac{\sum m_i x_i}{\sum m_i}$. cm of a system moves as if all external forces act there. fireworks: cm keeps following the parabola even after it explodes
