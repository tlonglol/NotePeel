## Uniform Circular Motion
Speed constant but velocity changes (direction) so there IS acceleration. Centripetal accel $a_c = \frac{v^2}{r}$ points toward center. Centripetal force $F_c = \frac{mv^2}{r}$, not a new force - its whatever provides it (tension, gravity, friction, normal). Period T, $v = \frac{2\pi r}{T}$.

## Examples
- car on flat curve: friction is centripetal force, max speed $v = \sqrt{\mu g r}$
- ball on string vertical circle: at top $T + mg = \frac{mv^2}{r}$, min speed when T = 0
- centrifugal force is NOT real, its inertia in a rotating frame

## Newtons Law of Gravitation
$F = G\frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$, $G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}$. Inverse square law: double the distance -> 1/4 the force. g on earths surface $= \frac{GM}{R^2} = 9.8$.

## Orbits
- gravity provides centripetal force: $\frac{GMm}{r^2} = \frac{mv^2}{r}$ -> $v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}$
- orbital speed does not depend on satellite mass
- Keplers 3rd law $T^2 \propto r^3$
- astronauts feel weightless b/c theyre in free fall, not b/c no gravity
