## IMFs vs Bonds
Intermolecular forces are BETWEEN molecules, much weaker than the covalent bonds WITHIN a molecule. IMFs determine boiling point, melting point, viscosity, surface tension. Stronger IMF = higher boiling point.

## Types (weakest to strongest)
- London dispersion forces - temporary dipoles from electron movement, present in ALL molecules, only force in nonpolar ones, stronger w/ more electrons (bigger molecules)
- Dipole-dipole - between polar molecules, partial + attracts partial -
- Hydrogen bonding - special strong dipole-dipole, H bonded to N, O, or F (FON!), why water has high boiling pt and ice floats
- Ion-dipole - ion and polar molecule, ex Na+ dissolving in water

## Water Properties from H bonding
- high specific heat, high surface tension, cohesion & adhesion
- ice less dense than liquid water (hexagonal lattice)
- universal solvent for polar/ionic stuff, like dissolves like
