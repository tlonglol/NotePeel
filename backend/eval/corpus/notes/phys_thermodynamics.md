## Heat vs Temperature
Temperature = average kinetic energy of particles. Heat = energy transferred b/c of temp difference, flows hot to cold. $Q = mc\Delta T$, c = specific heat (water 4186 J/kg C, high). Phase changes: $Q = mL$, latent heat, temp stays constant during a phase change.

## Heat Transfer
- conduction: direct contact, metals good conductors
- convection: fluid movement, hot air rises
- radiation: EM waves, no medium needed, the sun

## Laws of Thermodynamics
- 0th: if A and B are each in thermal equilibrium w/ C then A and B are in equilibrium (defines temperature)
- 1st: $\Delta U = Q - W$, energy conservation, internal energy change = heat added minus work done BY the gas
- 2nd: entropy of an isolated system never decreases, heat cant flow cold to hot on its own, no engine is 100% efficient
- 3rd: entropy approaches a minimum as T approaches 0 K

## Heat Engines
efficiency $e = \frac{W}{Q_h} = 1 - \frac{Q_c}{Q_h}$. Carnot (ideal) efficiency $1 - \frac{T_c}{T_h}$ in Kelvin. pg 2 has the PV diagram examples
