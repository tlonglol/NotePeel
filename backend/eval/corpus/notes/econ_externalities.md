## Market Failure
When the free market does not produce the socially optimal quantity. Main causes: externalities, public goods, monopoly power, information asymmetry.

## Externalities
Cost or benefit to a third party not involved in the transaction.
- negative externality (pollution, secondhand smoke): market OVERproduces, marginal social cost > marginal private cost. fix w/ a Pigouvian tax equal to the external cost, regulation, or cap and trade permits
- positive externality (education, vaccines, flu shots): market UNDERproduces, marginal social benefit > private benefit. fix w/ subsidies
- Coase theorem: if property rights are clear and transaction costs low, private parties can bargain to the efficient outcome on their own

## Public Goods
Two properties: non rival (my use doesnt reduce yours) and non excludable (cant stop people who dont pay). Ex national defense, lighthouses, fireworks. Free rider problem -> private market wont provide enough, so government provides them via taxes.
- private goods: rival and excludable (pizza)
- common resources: rival but not excludable (fish in the ocean) -> tragedy of the commons, overuse
- club goods: excludable but non rival (streaming service, toll road)
